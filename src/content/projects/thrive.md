# THRIVE — Unwind IPA  
### Transparent Brewing. Transparent Design.

## Overview
Thrive — Unwind IPA is a university-developed beverage designed around radical transparency: minimal ingredients, clear formulation, and honest communication. This case study explores how transparency became both the product promise and the core UX narrative, resulting in a digital experience built to inspire trust, calm, and curiosity.

---

## My Approach
The guiding principle behind Thrive – Unwind IPA is radical transparency. From day one, the vision wasn’t to *claim* honesty — it was to *prove it*. The website and brand system were designed to dissolve skepticism, remove guesswork, and build confidence through openness.  

Every interaction, headline, and visual aims to make users feel:  
**“You can relax — you know exactly what you’re drinking.”**

---

## Concept & Story
Thrive uses transparency not as visual gimmick, but as a **philosophical and emotional foundation**.  

### Narrative pillars:
- **We have nothing to hide** — minimal ingredients, clear visual reveals  
- **Transparency creates safety** — clarity equals peace of mind  
- **When trust is earned, calm begins** — transparency = freedom to unwind  

The landing experience functions as a ritual: ingredient reveals, process breakdowns, and soothing transitions guide users through a journey of trust and release — mirroring the act of unwinding.

---

## Visual Language
The visual identity avoids craft-beer stereotypes and instead leans into wellness and clarity:

- Clean, soft interface with understated gradients  
- Calming scroll-based transitions & GSAP micro-motion  
- University-lab trust accents: x-ray ingredient overlays, data callouts  
- Transparent can visuals as the hero motif  
- Human-centered lifestyle photography — calm, real, effortless  

The result is a **fresh hybrid**: part research-backed product, part relaxation ritual.

---

## Interaction & Experience
Every section invites participation and discovery, reinforcing openness:

- Scroll-to-reveal ingredients  
- AR x-ray effect to “see inside” the can  
- Process walkthroughs that feel meditative  
- “Unwind Rituals” interface — guidance meets storytelling  
- Soft feedback and micro-motion to support a calm flow  

Instead of selling, the interface **reassures, reveals, and invites trust**.

---

## Process

1. **Moodboards**
   - Transparency in nature, skincare, wellness, minimal ingredient brands  
   - Avoided typical beer tropes (woodgrain, neon hops, heavy type)  

2. **Experience Mapping**
   - Storyboarded the “unwind ritual” and transparency checkpoints  

3. **Wireframes & UX**
   - Defined trust moments: ingredients, source clarity, research framing  

4. **Visual + Motion Design**
   - Built every layer in Figma
   - Iterated interaction timing & breathing space for calm usability  

5. **Prototype**
   - Transparent packaging showcase  
   - Ingredient reveal system  
   - Subtle AR elements

The finished experience is:

Built with intention, clarity, and honesty  

It doesn't simply talk about transparency —  
it **lets users feel it**.
