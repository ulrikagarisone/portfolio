# Red Cross Flanders – User Experience Analysis

## Overview
This project focuses on analyzing and improving the **blood donation experience** at the Belgian Red Cross of Flanders. Blood transfusions are critical for surgeries, accidents, and childbirth, so ensuring a smooth donation process is essential. The goal of this assignment was to **document the donor experience, create a user journey map, identify pain points, and propose solutions** for better engagement and conversion.

## Key Goals
- Understand the blood donation process from a donor’s perspective.
- Identify pain points and opportunities to improve the donor experience.
- Map the complete **AIDA funnel** for donors.
- Propose solutions for leaky funnels to increase conversion and satisfaction.
- Document the full UX process for client presentation.

---

## Approach / Process

### 1. Field Observation
- Joined a group of 3 to **donate blood or plasma** at a local donor center.
- Documented **every step**: pre-planning, navigation, check-in, interaction with staff, donation, and post-donation follow-up.
- Captured insights through:
  - Photos (with consent)
  - Sketches
  - Notes and voice recordings
- Observed **other donors** while respecting privacy.

### 2. User Journey Mapping
- Used documentation from field observations to **create a full AIDA-based journey map**.
- Included:
  - Actions: Every step from deciding to donate to leaving the center.
  - Thoughts & Emotions: Empathized with donors’ feelings throughout the process.
  - Touchpoints: Both offline and digital interactions were noted.
  - Pain points & Opportunities: Framed as **HMW questions** to identify areas for improvement.
- Tools: Figjam for collaborative journey mapping.

### 3. Leaky Funnel & Solution Ideation (Extra)
- Identified where the **conversion rate drops** (pre-registration, arrival, information gaps).
- Brainstormed solutions to increase participation and reduce drop-off:
  - Simplified online registration flow
  - Reminder notifications
  - Educational content on donation benefits
- Added a **row of solutions** to the journey map for clear client recommendations.

### 4. Behance Presentation
- Included:
  - Project introduction and team members
  - Explanation of a user journey map
  - Embedded Figjam user journey map
  - Pain points and opportunities with HMW framing
  - Top solution for the leaky funnel
- Delivered a clear, organized, and visually appealing portfolio piece for client review.

## Outcomes & Learnings
- Created a **detailed, comprehensive user journey map** for Red Cross donors.
- Identified actionable pain points and opportunities to improve donor experience.
- Learned to combine **field research, empathy mapping, and UX design thinking** in a real-world context.
- Strengthened skills in:
  - User observation and qualitative research
  - AIDA-based journey mapping
  - Collaborative tools: Miro and Figjam
  - Presenting UX insights effectively for stakeholders

**Key takeaway:** Mapping the donor experience highlights both emotional and practical barriers, offering clear opportunities for increasing engagement and satisfaction.
