# Craftzing UX Research Collaboration

## Overview
This project is my **XD3 final assignment** in collaboration with Craftzing. We focus on **user-centered design** and **digital experiences** to address real-world challenges.  

Our team is exploring **how immigrant families with young children (0–6 years old) navigate screen time management** amidst cultural differences and social pressures.

---

## Research Focus
We aim to understand **how immigrant parents in Belgium make decisions about their children’s screen time**, and how **social influences—peers, family, and community norms—impact their confidence and daily choices**.  

The goal is to identify the **emotional and practical challenges** these families face to design **supportive and culturally sensitive digital tools**.

---

## Research Methods & Data Collection

### Surveys
We deployed **online surveys** targeting immigrant parents of children aged 0–6 to gather both quantitative and qualitative data.  
Survey topics included:  
- Family demographics and immigration history  
- Screen time habits and challenges  
- Social pressures and community influences  
- Confidence in decision-making and coping strategies  

### Cultural Probes
To capture richer insights, we designed **cultural probes** inviting participants to share daily experiences through **diaries, drawings, and creative exercises**.  

---

## Key Learnings So Far
- **Cultural Differences:** Parents often balance stricter norms from their home countries with more relaxed Belgian practices, causing internal conflict.  
- **Social Influence:** Advice and judgments from other parents, both in-person and online, significantly impact parental confidence.  
- **Routine Importance:** Clear rules and predictable routines help manage conflict and reduce stress.  
- **Support Needs:** Parents want community support, clear guidance, and tools to feel confident without stigma.
