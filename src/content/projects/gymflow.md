# Gym Website Redesign for 50+ Audience

## Overview
This project focused on redesigning a gym website specifically for the **50+ age group**, guided by our persona **Annelies**. The old gym had a generic and uninspiring website, so the goal was to create a **digital experience that is engaging, informative, and motivating** for older adults.  

The final website addresses the unique needs, motivations, and fears of older users, while encouraging exercise, social connection, and confidence in participating in fitness activities.

---

## My Approach

### 1. Research
I started by analyzing **other gym and fitness websites** to identify:  
- Essential content and features for older users  
- Communication strategies that appeal to 50+ audiences  
- Opportunities to make the experience more accessible, motivating, and engaging  

This research informed the **minimal and user-specific content** for the website.

### 2. Ideation & Brainstorming
I brainstormed features tailored to Annelies and similar users:  
- **Fitness quiz** to find the best classes  
- **Member success stories** to inspire motivation  
- **Challenges outside the gym** for engagement  
- **Community group** via social media (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube)  
- **Fitness tracking tools** with advice and guidance  
- **Calendar showing class participation** to plan visits  

These ideas were prioritized based on relevance and value for the target audience.

### 3. Low-Fidelity Wireframes & User Testing
I created **quick & dirty prototypes** of the main pages and tested them with users **representing the persona Annelies**.

**Testing scenarios:**  
1. **First-time user:** Navigating the website to find classes and resources  
2. **Feature-specific tasks:** Completing the fitness quiz or checking class availability  

Testing was documented with **mobile videos** to capture usability, clarity, and content relevance.

### 4. High-Fidelity Wireframes
Based on user feedback, I developed **high-fidelity wireframes** for **four main pages**:  
- **Homepage:** Hub with featured classes, community highlights, and navigation  
- **Community Page:** Member stories, challenges, and social engagement  
- **About Page:** Gym philosophy and approach tailored for Annelies  
- **Problems Page:** Addressing fears, physical limitations, and solutions  

The wireframes use **clear hierarchy, annotations, and a tone of voice** that resonates with the 50+ audience.

---

## Persona – Annelies
- **Age:** 64  
- **Occupation:** Retired primary school teacher  
- **Goals:** Improve health, stay active, meet new people, challenge herself  
- **Fears & Frustrations:** Physical limitations, lack of guidance, embarrassment, inflexible scheduling  
- **Digital Habits:** Uses Facebook & WhatsApp, reads health and fitness articles, occasionally follows online fitness videos, tracks steps with a pedometer app

## Key Takeaways
- User-centered testing is essential for designing for a specific demographic  
- Older adults value **community, guidance, and reassurance** in fitness services  
- Iterative design and testing lead to **clarity, usability, and motivation** for users
