# Click-Driven Game – Star Symphony

## Overview
Star Symphony is an interactive, rhythm-based clicker game. Players take on the role of a cosmic conductor, orchestrating stars in constellations. Clicking in rhythm earns "cosmic vibrancy" (points) to process wishes and expand the celestial orchestra.

**Key Goals:**
- Create a unique rhythm-based clicker game
- Demonstrate coding, UX, and motion design skills
- Implement responsive feedback and micro-interactions

## Features
- **Rhythm-based clicking:** Timing affects points and harmony meter
- **Upgrades & items:** Boost earnings and unlock new stars
- **Constellations:** Form glowing patterns when stars are purchased
- **Visual feedback & animations:** Pulsing stars, radial bursts, floating particles
- **Technical:** DOM manipulation, arrow-function event handlers, forms, localStorage, setInterval/setTimeout, CSS animations

**Live demo:** [Play Star Symphony](https://ulrikagarisone.be/click/)

## Approach / Concept
- **Theme:** Cosmic conductor maintaining harmony in the universe
- **Twist:** Rhythm timing is key—perfect clicks increase harmony and points; off-beat clicks reduce them
- **User Flow:** Click → earn points → buy stars → process wishes → form constellations
- **Design:** Deep space blues/purples, gold/silver stars, cosmic pastels; fonts: Orbitron (titles), Quicksand (text)
- **Technical Highlights:** Responsive 5×2 CSS grid, timestamp-based rhythm detection, dynamic constellation rendering, animated harmony meter

## Skills Demonstrated
- Interaction & motion design

- UX thinking and user feedback design
- Problem-solving and iterative prototyping

## Outcome & Learnings
- Built a fully interactive rhythm-based game with upgrades and automated progress
