# Don't Mow May – Digital Campaign Concept

## Overview
**Don't Mow May** is a digital concept designed to raise awareness about the ecological benefits of letting grass grow during May. The campaign targets Flemish gardeners and encourages them to cut their lawns less, promoting biodiversity and sustainable practices. By focusing on the digital experience, this project combines research, ideation, storytelling, and visual design to create an engaging, informative, and motivating user journey.

**Live project & documentation:** [Behance: Don’t Mow May](https://www.behance.net/gallery/213638307/Dont-Mow-May)

---

## Key Goals
- Inform, stimulate, and activate Flemish residents to mow less during May.
- Develop a creative digital experience that integrates seamlessly into a larger national campaign.
- Apply structured UX and design thinking processes: HMW questioning, brainstorming, solution selection, storyboarding, and pitch preparation.
- Create visually compelling content that balances education and engagement.

---

## Approach / Process

### 1. Understanding the Problem
- Conducted background research on the environmental impact of traditional lawns.
- Defined the problem using a **How Might We (HMW)** question:  
  *“How might we inspire Flemish residents to mow less and embrace biodiversity in their gardens?”*
- Identified key insights:  
  - Most gardeners are unaware of the ecological consequences of frequent mowing.  
  - Small, actionable steps can significantly improve biodiversity.  
  - Digital touchpoints offer the chance to educate, engage, and motivate behavior change.

### 2. Brainstorming & Ideation
- Applied **three brainstorming techniques** in Miro: mind-mapping, SCAMPER, and “crazy eights” to generate a wide range of ideas.
- Diverged widely to explore both conventional and playful solutions.
- Converged on the **top three ideas** based on feasibility, impact, and creativity:
  1. **Bee Tracker App:** Gamified experience showing pollinators attracted to your garden.  
  2. **Virtual Garden Challenge:** Users pledge to mow less and track garden biodiversity digitally.  
  3. **Augmented Reality Lawn Scanner:** Visualizes the ecological potential of your uncut lawn.

### 3. Solution Selection & Sketches
- Selected the **Virtual Garden Challenge** as the final concept.
- Developed sketches and wireframes demonstrating user interaction, storytelling, and visual feedback loops.
- Key features:
  - Simple onboarding and pledge system.
  - Digital feedback on the impact of mowing less.
  - Integration with social sharing to amplify participation.

### 4. Solution Storyboard
- Created a detailed storyboard illustrating the user's journey:  
  **Discover → Pledge → Track → Share → Reflect**
- Highlighted visual storytelling elements, user motivations, and emotional engagement points.
- Designed to be intuitive, playful, and educational.

### 5. Pitch Preparation
- Designed supporting slides in Canva for a 90-second pitch.
- Focused on clearly communicating the problem, concept, and user value.
- Prepared visuals to highlight the idea’s originality, engagement potential, and ecological impact.

---

## Design & Visual Style
- Mood: Fresh, playful, and approachable while reflecting ecological responsibility.
- Colors: Greens, earthy tones, and bright accents for engagement points.
- Typography: Clean, readable fonts for instructions and storytelling.
- Visual elements: Illustrations of pollinators, uncut grass patches, and gamified feedback mechanisms.
- Tools used: Figma (wireframes, storyboards, styleboards), Miro (brainstorming, HMW, ideation).

## Outcome & Learnings
- Developed a fully thought-out digital concept ready for integration into a national campaign.
- Created detailed documentation of ideation, sketches, storyboards, and pitch materials.
- Strengthened skills in:  
  - UX research and problem framing  
  - Creative brainstorming and solution selection  
  - Storyboarding and visual storytelling  
  - Digital campaign conceptualization  

**Key takeaway:** Small behavioral nudges, when combined with engaging digital experiences, can meaningfully influence sustainable actions.
