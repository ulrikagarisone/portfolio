# No Reception in Grocery Store, Stranded Without Maps

## Context
**Location:** Maxima supermarket, afternoon  
**Situation:** Customer enters store, loses mobile data, can't load shopping list app or store map.

## The UX Problem
Modern shoppers rely on phones for:
- Shopping lists (digital apps)
- Store maps and product location
- Price comparison
- Mobile payment
- Recipe lookup while shopping

Losing signal = losing essential shopping tools.

**Observed scenario:**
- Customer enters store with phone
- Signal drops to zero
- Can't load shopping list app
- Can't find products (large unfamiliar store)
- Can't check recipes for missing ingredients
- Frustrated, inefficient shopping
- May need to abandon purchase if mobile-pay only

## Why It Matters
**User impact:**
- Increased shopping time (wandering aimlessly)
- Forgotten items (no list access)
- Can't use loyalty apps or coupons
- Payment issues (mobile wallet needs connection)

**Business impact:**
- Lost sales (frustrated shoppers leave)
- Lower basket sizes (forget items)
- Reduced app engagement
- Poor digital experience ratings

## Root Causes
1. **Building materials** block cell signals (concrete, metal)
2. **No guest WiFi** provided
3. **No signal boosters** installed
4. **Apps don't work offline** (poor design)
5. **No physical backup** (paper maps, product lookup kiosks)

## Solution Directions

### Immediate Fix
- Install **free, fast guest WiFi**
- Clear signage at entrance: "Free WiFi: SSID / Password"
- No registration required (friction = abandoned use)

### Better Solution
**Hybrid digital-physical:**
- WiFi + physical backup systems
- Paper shopping lists at entrance
- Product lookup kiosks (touch-screen)
- Staff tablets to help customers locate items
- QR codes on shelves → product info (cached)

## Best Solution
**Seamless connectivity + offline-first apps:**
- Offline mode: Cache last shopping list
- Pre-download store maps on entry
- Sync when connection returns
- Works fully offline for essential features

## UX Principles Applied
- **Robustness** — Systems should work in all conditions
- **Graceful degradation** — Core features work offline
- **User control** — Don't trap users in dead zones
- **Continuity** — Seamless digital-physical transitions

---

**Key Takeaway:** Don't let infrastructure limitations break user experience. Design for worst-case scenarios. Offline isn't optional—it's a requirement.
